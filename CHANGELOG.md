# 0.0.1

### Added

* **Party Zones.** A new **Party Zone** region behavior: player tokens inside a zone can't leave it, and tokens outside every zone can only move into one. Touching or overlapping zones count as one area, holes count as outside, zones follow the scene's Levels, and the GM is never restricted.
* **Shocking edges.** A player's token that tries to leave a zone walks up to the edge, flashes, shakes and sparks while the barrier ripples where it was hit and a zap plays for everyone, and is thrown one grid space back inside. Choose the sound — or none — in the module settings.
* **Draw Party Zone tool.** One drag in the Regions controls creates a gold zone that every player can see, with the behavior already attached. Select a zone and draw again to grow it.
* **Lift a zone without deleting it.** Disable the zone's **Party Zone** behavior to free the players, and enable it again when you need it.
